Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any riding experience to book a lesson?

Not at all. Linda teaches complete beginners every week and particularly welcomes nervous first-timers. Just let her know your experience level when you book.

What should I wear for my first lesson?

Long trousers (jeans or jodhpurs) and flat-soled boots with a small heel. Avoid shorts or loose clothing. Helmets and body protectors are provided — no need to buy your own for your first visit.

What happens if it rains?

Littlefield Manor has a fully enclosed, all-weather arena — so lessons proceed in almost any conditions. In genuinely severe weather Linda will contact you to rearrange.

I'm nervous around horses — is that a problem?

Absolutely not — nervous riders are Linda's speciality. She'll spend as much time as you need getting comfortable on the ground with the horses before any riding begins. There's never any pressure.

Can I buy a block of lessons?

Contact Linda directly to discuss lesson packages and regular bookings — she's happy to arrange regular slots that suit your schedule.

Any more questions?

Linda is happy to talk it through

If you'd like to discuss which lesson type is right for you, or you have a question about the school — just get in touch. No commitment needed.
