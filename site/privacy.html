Your Information

Privacy notice

What we do with the details you share when you get in touch, and what this website does when you visit it.

Last updated 21 September 2026

Who we are

Littlefield Manor Riding School, Aldershot Road, Worplesdon, Guildford GU3 3HJ. We decide how the personal information described here is used, which makes us its "controller" under UK data protection law.

To contact us about your information, text or call 07973 302316 or email lindalucas@live.co.uk.

When you get in touch or book

When you text, call or email us, we have your name, your phone number or email address, and whatever you tell us about the rider, such as their age, riding experience or weight. If you're booking for a child, that includes the details you give us about them.

We use this only to answer your questions, arrange and run lessons, and keep riders safe. The law allows this because you've asked us about a lesson or booked one, and because we have a legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.

We don't sell your details, and we don't pass them to anyone for marketing. Your messages are held by the phone and email services we use to receive them.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you're riding with us or discussing a booking, and delete them once we no longer need them to arrange lessons or deal with any question about a lesson.

This website

  • No cookies or tracking. The website doesn't set cookies, doesn't use analytics, and doesn't follow you around the web. That's why there's no cookie banner.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (including your IP address) to keep the site secure and working.
  • Fonts, map and page features. When a page loads, your browser also connects to Google Fonts for the lettering, Cloudflare for a small animation library, and our database provider for the latest prices and photos. The map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps. Each of these receives your IP address as part of delivering what you asked for. Google's use is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Just text or email us using the details above. It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF, on 0303 123 1113, or at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top of the page.
