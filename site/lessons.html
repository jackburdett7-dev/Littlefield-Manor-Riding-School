What We Offer

Riding Lessons
For Every Level

From your very first time in the saddle to show jumping and cross-country — Linda teaches every lesson personally in a calm, encouraging environment.

Our Approach

Learning at
your own pace

At Littlefield Manor, there's no rush. Linda works with you at whatever pace feels right — whether you need ten minutes just to get comfortable around the horses, or you're eager to canter on your first session.

Every lesson takes place in our fully enclosed 25×60m all-weather outdoor arena, meaning we can ride in almost any conditions. Nervous riders are not just tolerated here — they're our speciality.

  • All safety equipment provided — no need to bring anything
  • Horses available for riders up to 15 stone
  • Off-road hacking available for experienced riders
  • Children welcome from age 6

Disciplines

What you'll learn to ride

Linda teaches across all major English disciplines, tailored to your level and goals.

🏇

Classical Riding

The foundation of all good horsemanship — balance, position, and communication with your horse. Suitable for all levels.

🚧

Show Jumping

Learn to jump with confidence and style. Linda introduces jumping gradually, building your nerve and technique at the right pace.

🌿

Cross-Country & Hacking

For more experienced riders — take your riding outside the arena with off-road hacking through the Surrey countryside.
