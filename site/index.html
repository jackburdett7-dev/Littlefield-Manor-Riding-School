Meet Your Instructor

The instructor who stays with you

I'm Linda. I've been running Littlefield Manor for over two decades, and I still teach every single lesson myself. I made that choice early on — and I've never changed it. When you come to this yard, you build a relationship with me, with the horses, and with the place. That continuity is what turns beginners into confident riders.

My speciality is nervous riders and first-timers. If you've been putting riding off because you're worried you won't be good enough, or you're scared of the horses — please come and say hello first. There's never any pressure to get on until you're ready.

  • BHS (British Horse Society) Qualified Instructor
  • TTT (Train the Trainer) Certified
