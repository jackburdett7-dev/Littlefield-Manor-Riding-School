For Horse-Mad Children

Own a Pony Day

For one day, your child gets to feel like they own their own pony. They'll learn to groom, tack up, feed, and care for their pony — then spend time riding in the arena. It's a full, hands-on experience that most children talk about for months afterwards.

Own a Pony Day Full day £90

Weight limit: 75kg. Ask us about availability.

What's included

  • Grooming and tacking up under our guidance
  • Feeding and learning about horse care
  • Riding time in the enclosed arena
  • All safety equipment provided
  • A day they'll remember forever

Give them a birthday they'll never forget. Littlefield Manor riding parties are the perfect alternative to the usual bowling or cinema trip — and all the children will be talking about it at school on Monday.
