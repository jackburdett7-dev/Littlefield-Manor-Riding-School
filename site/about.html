In Linda's Own Words

"Horses changed
my life — they can change yours too."

I'm Linda, and I've been running Littlefield Manor for over two decades. Horses have been part of my life for as long as I can remember — and the more I rode, the more I realised I wanted to share that with other people. Teaching people to ride, and watching them grow in confidence, never gets old.

I hold a BHS qualification and Train the Trainer certification, but honestly what I'm proudest of is my reputation for patience. The riders I love most are the nervous ones — the adults who've always wanted to try but never felt safe enough, the children who approach cautiously and leave with a huge smile. Those lessons matter most to me.

At Littlefield Manor, every lesson is taught by me personally. You won't be handed to a different instructor each week. You'll build a relationship with me, with the horses, and with the yard — and that consistency is what makes the real difference. If you're wondering whether riding is for you, come and say hello. There's no commitment, and the kettle's always on.

The School

Our facilities

🏟️

All-Weather Arena

25×60m fully enclosed outdoor arena. We ride in almost any weather — sessions are rarely cancelled.

🌿

Off-Road Hacking

For experienced riders, we offer hacking on quiet off-road routes through the Worplesdon countryside.

🐎

Suitable Horses

Horses available for riders up to 15 stone. A calm, well-matched horse makes all the difference to a lesson.
